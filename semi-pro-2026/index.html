Melrose Park

Toretto

Edwards, Mitchell, Durant, Towns and Amen are a formidable top five, and Bane plus Knueppel give rare GF flexibility. Gobert covers C absences; Durant's age is the main availability concern, but this is the deepest roster so far.

Guildford Wst

drewson

Johnson, Brown and Booker cover valuable forward and guard minutes, while Sabonis at 49 and Sarr at 95 create a strong center plan. Boozer and Buzelis add upside without forcing the whole roster to depend on rookies.

03 / 10A−

Abbotsford

*dazzz

Tatum and Flagg are an excellent forward pairing, Harden and Murray supply guard creation, and Duren, Holmgren and Queen offer center coverage. Tatum's return, Dejounte Murray's recovery and a few young rotation bets add more uncertainty than the names alone suggest.

04 / 10A−

Marsden Park

*RayzoR

Cade, Brunson and Reaves provide a strong, fairly deep guard core; Şengün and Jackson are an efficient C/FC pairing. Markkanen, Murphy and Daniels cover forward, though some guard talent may be squeezed out of the 96-minute allotment on full-strength days.

05 / 10B

Phoenix Suns

edgeofreason

Jokić supplies the league's safest center advantage, while Avdija, Banchero and Randle give genuine forward output. Clingan completes the center rotation; Castle, a returning Lillard and rookie Peterson leave guard availability uncertain.

06 / 10B

Ahrensburg

Brad271

Gilgeous-Alexander and Giddey are excellent guard anchors, with Siakam and Ausar supporting the forward lane. Embiid and Zion raise the ceiling but also the missed-game risk; Allen and Reid cushion C/FC, while three pure guards leave little G insurance yet.

07 / 10B−

PromiseLand

Sachin

Maxey, White and Fox can fill G, and Bam with Hartenstein secures C, so the position map works. The grade is held down by an unusually large availability bet on Kawhi, LeBron and Butler, with only nine picks made so far.

08 / 10B−

Hamburg

Maschine

Giannis and Davis offer tremendous CF production, and Trae gives dependable assists, but Morant, Davis and Robinson stack health uncertainty; ESPN projects Davis for 61 games after just 20 in 2025–26. Franz is the only pure F drafted, making forward coverage especially vulnerable when a CF is absent or needed at center.

09 / 10B−

Casual

*aussieburger

Dončić, Barnes and Mobley are a strong beginning, and Ball gives a high guard ceiling. Ware, Edey and Sharpe form a sensible rotation of lower-minute centers; the remaining concerns are Jerome's price and availability, Edey's health, and limited proven F depth.

10 / 10C+

Marcus

traazi

Wembanyama is an exceptional anchor and Zubac provides C insurance, with several useful forwards behind them. Curry, Haliburton and Irving are the only three guards drafted, and all carry substantial age or return-from-injury uncertainty; guard minutes are the clearest threat to this roster.
