02 / THE VERDICT

The final report card.

Relative grades for all ten complete rosters, weighing position coverage, value and projected availability before opening night.

AS OF 19 OCT '26 / FINAL DRAFT
01 / 10A+

Melrose Park

Toretto

Edwards, Mitchell, Durant and Towns form an elite top four, with Amen, Bane and Knueppel adding scoring and GF flexibility. Quickley strengthens G coverage and Okongwu is superb center insurance, though the crowded perimeter and Durant's age keep this from being a risk-free roster.

02 / 10A

Guildford Wst

drewson

Johnson, Brown, Booker and Sabonis give Guildford a strong, balanced core; Ingram at 110 and Kyshawn George at 111 deepen the forward and GF lanes at attractive prices. Sarr backs up Sabonis, and the rookie upside in Boozer and Buzelis is a bonus rather than the whole plan.

03 / 10A−

Abbotsford

*dazzz

Tatum and Flagg anchor a high-upside forward group, while Harden, Dejounte Murray, Coby White, Powell and Green now provide ample guard coverage. Duren, Holmgren and Queen handle C, but Tatum's and Murray's recoveries plus several young players introduce real games-played variance.

04 / 10A−

Marsden Park

*RayzoR

Cade, Brunson and Reaves offer top-class guard minutes, and Şengün, Jackson and a late Vučević form a sensible center plan. Markkanen, Murphy, Daniels and Jaquez fill F or flex there; the main cost of this depth is that some healthy guards will be squeezed by the 96-minute cap.

05 / 10B+

PromiseLand

Sachin

Maxey, Fox, White and Edgecombe cover G, while Bam, Hartenstein and Nurkić cover C and OG Anunoby gives the forwards a strong late addition. Kawhi, LeBron and Butler make the ceiling alluring, but their combined age and injury risk could leave a lot of those forward minutes in less productive hands.

06 / 10B+

Ahrensburg

Brad271

Gilgeous-Alexander and Giddey lead a guard group that now has Garland and Nembhard for reliable depth; Siakam, Ausar and Reid help keep F populated. Allen and Drummond are practical center cover for Embiid, but Embiid's and Zion's projected availability plus rookie Steinbach still put a ceiling on the grade.

07 / 10B

Casual

*aussieburger

Dončić, Barnes and Mobley are an excellent core, and LaMelo Ball adds star-level G upside. Ware, Edey and Sharpe are a sensible combined center plan at their individual minutes, while Jerome's fifth-round price, Edey's health and thin proven F depth make the supporting cast less secure.

08 / 10B−

Phoenix Suns

edgeofreason

Jokić is the best single foundation, and Avdija, Banchero and Randle offer a productive forward group; Clingan and Queta give normal center cover. The final picks added two more pure forwards and a center, leaving Castle, returning Lillard, rookie Peterson and Avdija's GF flexibility to carry a fragile guard plan.

09 / 10B−

Marcus

traazi

Wembanyama is a league-shifting anchor, Zubac and Mara cover C, and Jalen Williams, Miller, George and Barrett give the forward lane options. The guard group is Curry, two Achilles returnees in Haliburton and Irving, and rookie Fears: its ceiling is real, but missed games or managed minutes could expose the 96-minute requirement quickly.

10 / 10C+

Hamburg

Maschine

Giannis and Davis are huge CF scorers, and Trae, Keyonte George and Kessler give the lineup real value. Davis, Morant and Robinson already carried games-played risk, while Simmons's post-pick Achilles tear removes a late GF option; Hezonja's uncertain NBA role leaves the forward depth thin behind Franz and the CF stars.
