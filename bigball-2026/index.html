02 / THE VERDICT

The final report card.

Relative grades for Bigball's ten completed rosters, weighing value, position coverage and expected availability.

AS OF 18 OCT '26 / DRAFT COMPLETE
01 / 10A

Springfield

fbannier

Cade, Tatum, Durant and Barnes are the league's most imposing first four, with White, Garland and Alexander-Walker covering G and Sarr, Turner, Jackson and Jaylin Williams providing frontcourt options. Tatum's return and the age or health of Durant, Garland and Lillard are real risks, but the roster has enough depth to absorb some missed games.

02 / 10A−

A-Town

Knut86

Maxey, Brunson and Bane handle the guard lanes while Şengün, Mobley and Gobert cover C; Murphy, Porter, Barrett and Bridges give the forwards several playable paths. It is a balanced, fairly dependable build with less reliance on one injury comeback than most rivals.

03 / 10A−

Bonn

Fibre123

Gilgeous-Alexander and Flagg are premium anchors, and Reaves at 50, Pritchard at 107 and the Bam/Okongwu/Queen center rotation are excellent values. Kawhi's projected games are the largest variable in an otherwise flexible roster.

04 / 10A−

Aussie

aussieburger

Jokić is a tremendous foundation and Giddey, Booker, Haliburton and Dejounte Murray give high-end guard production; Williams, Miller and Boozer make F competitive. The grade stops short of A because multiple guards are coming off major injuries or short seasons, and the younger forwards still have role uncertainty.

05 / 10B+

Ahrensburg

Finn + 1

Giannis, Avdija, Duren and Banchero create a powerful F/C base, with Hartenstein a normal center partner rather than a luxury pick. Herro is the only established pure-G producer here; Harper and Mitchell need to grow quickly, even with Avdija and Jaquez available as GF bridges.

06 / 10B+

White Men

BolBolBol

Dončić, Towns, Amen and Siakam can carry excellent daily output across all three lanes, with Zubac solid center cover. Irving's return, George's age and the speculative Acuff, Bailey and Simmons picks leave less trustworthy depth than the top four suggest.

07 / 10B

Ulaanbataar

*realarslan

Johnson, Mitchell, Harden and the Clingan/Sabonis center pair provide a high ceiling; Fox and Wagner add genuine quality. Ingram has no return timetable, and a guard-heavy bench means the 96 F minutes are less secure than the roster's total talent implies.

08 / 10B

Hamburg

Lukas_k

Edwards, Murray and Knueppel give flexible perimeter scoring, while Kessler and Allen sensibly cover Embiid's likely missed games. Brown at 12, Embiid at 42 and Butler at 59 spend premium picks on reduced roles or availability risks, leaving a wide range of possible outcomes.

09 / 10B−

Groningen

easy3pointz

Wembanyama and Holmgren form a formidable C/FC base, and Trae, Curry and Quickley can supply guard points. LeBron, Zion, Curry and the rookie Dybantsa make the F/G minutes unusually dependent on age, health and development; DeRozan helps the forward floor with the final pick.

10 / 10C+

Agathenburg

Maschine

Davis and Reid can flex between frontcourt lanes, and Edey plus Sharpe provide a sensible center rotation; Castle and Randle offer steadier production. The early Ball/Morant/Davis trio has the league's most concentrated games-played risk, while Wilson and Lendeborg are asked to shore up uncertain forward depth.
